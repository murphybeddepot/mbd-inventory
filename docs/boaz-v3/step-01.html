Lower pivot arms onto 1A and 1B

The bookcases swing on two pins. This step bolts the arm that carries each pin to the inside face of the lower bed-cabinet ends, before those ends become part of anything bigger.

Both ends are 476 × 178 × 19. They are a mirror pair, not two of the same part: laid inside-face up, 1A has its front edge on the left and 1B on the right.

Read the part first

Inside face

The face with three ∅5 pin holes in a column near the front edge. The arm goes on this face. The outside face is plain.

Bottom edge

The long edge with four ∅8 through-holes 20 mm in. The arm sits flush along it. 4″ apart, 74 from the front.

Top edge

New in V3: six ∅8 bores in the edge itself — three bolts, three dowels — for the joint to the upper end in step 2. Nothing goes in them yet.

Front edge

The short edgebanded edge nearest the pin holes. The pivot pin ends up past this edge, pointing up.

Do this

  1. Stand 1A on its bottom edge on a blanket, inside face toward you, front edge to your left. The three pin holes are now at the left, near the top.
  2. Set a pivot arm along the bottom edge on the inside face, tube flat against the panel, pin end past the front edge with the pin pointing up. Line up its four holes with the four ∅8 holes in the panel.
  3. Push the four bolts in from the outside face, through the panel and through the tube. Start a nylock nut on each by hand.
  4. Tighten with the hex key in the bolt and the socket on the nut until each countersunk head pulls itself flush into the outside face. That face gets covered by a side toe later, so nothing may stand proud.
  5. Repeat for 1B, mirrored: inside face toward you, front edge to your right, pin past the right edge.

Right and wrong

✓ Pin past the front, pointing up

Arm on the inside face, flush with the bottom edge, pin beyond the edgebanded front edge and pointing toward the top edge.

✗ Pin at the back

The arm fits the holes either way round. With the pin at the back edge the bookcase has nothing to swing on.

✗ Arm on the outside face

The holes go through, so the arm bolts up on the plain face too. The pin then sits outside the cabinet and the side toe cannot fit.

✗ Same hand twice

Two right ends: pins on the same side, one bookcase with no pivot. Check the pin-hole column is on the left for 1A, right for 1B.
