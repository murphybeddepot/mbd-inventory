Join the lower and upper ends

New in V3: the lower end and the upper end are bolted to each other — three cams, three bolts, three dowels down the seam — and the bottom brace goes over the joint afterwards. In V2 the brace did this job alone.

Read the parts first

1D’s bottom edge

Three ∅15 cam holes on the inside face, 34 up from the edge, and six ∅8 bores in the edge itself. That edge meets 1A. The other end has the Top’s pin holes.

1A’s top edge

Six ∅8 bores, alternating: 13 deep at 60 / 204 / 348 from the front for the bolts, 16 deep at 132 / 276 / 420 for the dowels.

Front edges align

Both parts are drilled from the front edge. 1D is 495 deep to 1A’s 476, so with fronts in line it runs 19 past 1A at the back. That is correct.

Brace covers the cams

The brace sits over 1D’s three cams. Tighten the cams first; there is no reaching them once the brace is on.

Do this

  1. Cams into 1D and 1E. Drop a cam into each of the three ∅15 holes near the bottom edge, arrow pointing at the bottom edge. Same on 1E.
  2. Bolts and dowels into 1A’s top edge. Screw a ∅8 bolt into each 13-deep bore until its collar seats. Push a dowel into each 16-deep bore. A dowel in the wrong bore stands 3 mm proud — swap it.
  3. Lay 1A and 1D inside-face up, front edges in one line, pivot arm toward the middle. Lower 1D’s bottom edge onto 1A’s top edge so the bolts run into 1D and the dowels find their bores. Tap the joint closed with the mallet.
  4. Tighten the three cams with the #3 Phillips: clockwise, just past half a turn, until each is snug. Do not force past that.
  5. Bottom brace over the seam. Back edge on the back edge of 1A, sitting above the pivot arm. Eight 1¼″ screws through its pilots: four into 1A, four into 1D.
  6. Repeat for 1B and 1E, mirrored. You now have two end assemblies.

Animation

Right and wrong

✗ Dowel in a bolt bore

The 13-deep bores are for bolts. A dowel there stands 3 mm proud and 1D will not close on it. Bolts at 60 / 204 / 348, dowels between.

✓ Fronts in line, back steps out

Align the banded front edges. The 19 mm step at the back is by design; do not centre 1D on 1A.

✗ Brace before cams

The brace covers all three cams. If it goes on first the joint is never pulled tight, and the only fix is to take the brace off again.

Notes for the illustrator
